# Future Roadmap

## Current Product Focus

The hackathon MVP focuses only on:

> Digital financial onboarding, FNA automation and compliance readiness.

The short-term motor claims concept is intentionally deferred.

## Phase 1 — Hackathon MVP

### Financial Onboarding
- document upload;
- structured extraction;
- FNA data model;
- adviser verification;
- workbook population;
- compliance-readiness indicators.

### Outcome
Reduce repetitive adviser data entry while preserving human review.

## Phase 2 — Smart Motor Claims

Future functionality may include:

- mobile accident-scene wizard;
- GPS/location capture;
- vehicle-damage photo capture;
- registration and licence evidence;
- third-party details;
- police case number;
- voice statement;
- claim evidence-pack generation;
- insurer submission adapters where supported by approved integrations;
- real-time claim progress tracking;
- client notifications;
- adviser claim-workflow dashboard.

## Phase 3 — Connected Client Profile

A verified FNA client profile could later support:

- annual client reviews;
- document renewal reminders;
- policy and portfolio servicing;
- financial-goal tracking;
- consent renewal;
- claims-linked client history.

## Phase 4 — Enterprise Integrations

Potential future integrations may include:

- CRM;
- document management;
- e-signature;
- insurer APIs;
- banking/open-finance integrations;
- compliance systems;
- identity verification services.

## Pitch Positioning

The future roadmap should be mentioned briefly.

Recommended message:

> We are proving the financial-onboarding and FNA automation layer first. Once a verified client profile exists, the same platform can later support a digital short-term insurance claims journey and broader client servicing.

Do not spend MVP development time building Phase 2.
